# ChildcareOS Brand

Status: locked

## Position

ChildcareOS is calm operational infrastructure for childcare centers. It should feel dependable under pressure, clear from across a front desk, and serious without looking clinical.

## Palette

- Ink: `#17312D`
- Teal: `#0D9488`
- Deep teal: `#0B5D56`
- Coral: `#F47D4A`
- Cream: `#F7F3EA`
- White: `#FFFDFC`
- Success: `#187A55`
- Warning: `#A85B17`
- Danger: `#B83C32`

## Typography

- Display and UI: Geist Sans
- Operational data: Geist Mono
- Headlines are compact and direct. Body copy uses short sentences and concrete claims.

## Voice

- Calm, precise, and human.
- Never cute, sugary, or alarmist.
- Avoid vague AI language and unsupported safety claims.
